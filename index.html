Cognitive Bias Self-Assessment

A short behavioural reflection on how cognitive shortcuts may influence your thinking and decisions.

18 scenarios • approximately 7–10 minutes • self-reflection, not diagnosis

Before you begin

There are no right or wrong answers. Each scenario has several plausible responses. Choose the option that is closest to how you would actually approach the situation, rather than the response that sounds most ideal.

Answer based on what you actually tend to do, especially when making decisions quickly or under pressure, rather than what you think you should do.
